## GDExtension slice

- **Сборка расширения:** `cmake -S gdextension -B gdextension/build-ofport -G "Visual Studio 17 2022" -A x64 -DGODOTCPP_TARGET=template_release -DGPF_REPO="<GameplayFootball checkout>" -DGPF_BUILD="<GameplayFootball checkout>/build-ofport"`, затем `cmake --build gdextension/build-ofport --config Release --target open_football_gdext`. C++-библиотеки должны быть собраны из ветки `of_port` в `build-ofport` (Release x64); линкуется также `netlib.lib`.
- **Запуск:** `godot --path .` → тайл **Quick Match** (сцена матча требует пакет от прематча; прямой `res://scenes/gpf_slice.tscn` без пакета возвращает в выбор команд); данные — `GPF_DATA_ROOT` или свойство `data_root` (по умолчанию путь к `GameplayFootball-of_port/data`).
- **Структура сцены:** `gpf_slice.gd` — тонкий оркестратор (связывает части и раздаёт ввод; состояние частей не дублирует). Компоненты: `GpfActors` (тела/мяч/подписи/ретикул), `GpfEnvironment` (стадион/газон/свет), `GpfGoalNets` (рамы + C++-сетка), `GpfCameraRig`, `GpfHud` (счёт, фазы), `GpfSetPieceTakerMenu` (порт `SetPieceTakerPage`), `GpfReplay`, `GpfShootoutView`, `GpfMatchOver` (экраны конца матча) — все `scripts/match/gpf_*.gd`, `GpfAssets` — общий lookup материалов. Перерыв показывается дизайн-системным `PauseScreen` в режиме `open_break` (подпись периода + «BEGIN SECOND HALF»/«BEGIN 1ST EXTRA TIME»), сеть — тот же resume-vote.
- **Конец матча:** `GpfMatchOver` (`scripts/match/gpf_match_over.gd`, CanvasLayer) держит два экрана на общей базе `MatchOverScreen` (`scripts/ui/match_over_screen.gd`: скорборд `PauseScoreboard` + строка турнира `package.home.league` + 2×2 плиток + правая панель + `UiHintsBar`): **RESULT** (`scenes/match/match_result.tscn`, `MatchResultScreen`) и **DECISION** (`scenes/match/match_choice.tscn`, `MatchChoiceScreen`). Маршрутизация — в `gpf_slice` по снапшоту (`choice_needed`): `game_over` → RESULT, а брейк `phase_break_next_phase == 3` при равном счёте → DECISION (вместо брейк-экрана паузы); у тонкого клиента латча нет, период берётся из `match_phase`, поэтому экран тот же, только read-only. RESULT: живые `ADVANCE` (главное меню) и `RESTART MATCH` (тот же пакет; в сети disabled, чип `ONLINE LOBBY`), `MATCH HIGHLIGHTS`/`PERFORMANCE` и правая панель `PLAYER PERFORMANCE` — SOON; часы заменены на `FULL TIME`, у финала серии — подпись `PENALTIES x – y`. DECISION: `CLASSIC` → `continue_after_phase_break()`, `PENALTIES` → `start_shootout()`, `DRAW` → `debug_force_game_over()`, `GOLDEN GOAL` — SOON; решает хост/P1, в сети у клиента плитки заблокированы и на каждой в слоте чипа (где `SOON`) написано `HOST CHOOSING`. Конец матча приезжает снапшотом (`game_over` из `Match::IsGameOver`), поэтому клиент видит RESULT и после серии пенальти; сетевой хост после game over не перестаёт тикать — иначе финальный снапшот до клиентов не доезжает. Старые окна (`GpfHud` phase menu, `GpfShootoutView` КОНЕЦ МАТЧА) удалены. Тесты — `tests/check_match_result_ui.gd`, `tests/check_match_choice_ui.gd`, `tests/check_gpf_result_flow.gd`; стенд — состояния `RESULT`/`DECISION` в `ui_lab`.
- **Окружение:** `assets/models/gpf_stadium.glb` (31 материал, текстуры запечены из `test.ase`) и отдельный `gpf_pitch.glb` (4 чанка исходника, runtime merge в одну поверхность) кладутся под `Environment` (raw X→X, Y→Z, Z→Y, как у ворот); газон получает процедурную текстуру C++ (`generate_pitch_texture`) и тему `dry/wet/damp/snow` независимо от стадиона. Свет — `DirectionalLight3D` `Sun` с тенями + `FillLight`, ACES-тонмаппинг; `F1` циклически переключает Noon/Afternoon/Sunset (Night временно отключён). `V` переключает обзор стадиона. Ворота — импортированный `gpf_goals.glb` (белая рамка), сетка остаётся процедурной из C++ NetSim.
- **Подписи игроков:** над каждым полевым — billboard `Label3D` с фамилией из `get_player_ids`; управляемый подсвечен жёлтым, подписи скрываются в обзоре стадиона (`V`). Имена приходят из C++ моста (`String::utf8`), официальные лица без подписей.
- **Видеоповторы голов:** `gpf_slice.gd` копит ring buffer последних ~5 секунд поз акторов, мяча и камеры. Гол сначала отыгрывает оригинальное празднование; на `sig_OnExtendedReplayMoment` матч ставится на паузу (`GpfRuntimeNode.set_replay_active(true)`) и проигрывается буфер, зафиксированный на моменте гола, плюс ~3 секунды после пересечения линии (без полноценного празднования), с баннером `REPLAY`; `Space`/`Esc` — пропуск, после — возврат в live. Оригинальный extended replay не размораживает матч, пока идёт наш повтор.
- **Серия пенальти:** F3 (`debug_force_shootout`) из любого чистого состояния или автоматически при ничьей после 120' (referee вместо `GameOver`). Логика в C++ (`Match::StartShootout`/`ProcessShootout` в чекауте): 5+5 с досрочной остановкой, sudden death парами, монетка, одни случайные ворота; стороны команд переназначаются на каждый удар (`Team::GetSide` override), очередь — все активные игроки по `technical_shot*0.6 + mental_calmness*0.4`, тэйкер форсируется. Счёт/часы матча заморожены, кик-оффа и extended replay нет, не-участники на центральной линии. Исходы и таймаут 3 с — в C++; `sad_normal` бьющему за рядовой сейв/мимо, празднование 5 с только на решающем ударе. В сети вся серия тоже приходит снапшотом (net v21: стороны, фаза, счёт, журнал ударов) и применяется в `ApplyRemoteSnapshot`, иначе клиент строил side-relative камеру пенальти по неверной стороне и смотрел в лицо бьющему. HUD в срезе: кружки сверху/снизу (зелёный/красный/серый), баннеры GOAL/SAVE/MISS, fade 0.3/0.15/0.3; после решающего празднования — пауза, фиксированная broadcast-камера (C++, иначе scorer-камера замирала в трибуне) и экран результата `MatchResultScreen` (подпись `PENALTIES x – y`; `ADVANCE`/`RESTART MATCH`). Спек/план: `docs/superpowers/{specs,plans}/2026-10-29-penalty-shootout-gpf.md`, вики — `docs/wiki/порт-gdextension.md`; тест — `tests/check_gpf_shootout.gd`.
- **Каталог и выбор команд:** Godot сам читает `database.sqlite` **лениво** (без C++-каталога): `SqliteReader` — read-only table b-tree в GDScript (+ `count_indexed`), `TeamCatalog` открывает файл/валидирует таблицы и запрашивает страны/лиги/команды по требованию (кэши; `team_by_id` идёт точечным rowid-поиском, команды с <11 игроками отфильтровываются при листинге через `can_field_eleven`/`count_indexed` по индексам `players.team_id`/`nationalteam_id`, а при наличии `team_eligibility.json` — готовыми наборами (`tools/build_team_eligibility.py`, аналог SQL-запроса оригинала); крупные лиги без кэша — одним `count_index_values`, ready-проверка — страховка; состав/рейтинг — только выбранной команды), `TeamRatings` — формула звёзд/ATT/MID/DEF из `base_stat`, `UiAssets` — флаги/гербы/лого из `images_{countries,leagues,teams}` (импорт `tools/import_prototype_images.py`, обводка `tools/outline_league_logos.py`, индексы `tools/optimize_game_db.py`), `ShellState` — статический выбор Home/Away (не autoload, чтобы работал в headless `-s`). `scenes/controller_select.tscn` — порт `SideSelectPage`; `scenes/team_select.tscn` — **тонкий адаптер** общего `TeamSelectView` (`scenes/ui/team_select_view.tscn`): две карточки HOME/AWAY с блоками Country/Team/Competition, флагами/гербами, звёздами и ATT/MID/DEF; Ready-ряда нет — `A/Enter` на любом блоке, `B/Esc` снимает/назад; в 1P home → CPU away, в 2P обе панели параллельны. `V` убран, мышь инертна. По умолчанию (без сохранённого выбора) открывается пара FC Barcelona vs Real Madrid. Ready обеих сторон ведёт в Kit Select. Вики — `docs/wiki/каталог-команд.md`, `docs/wiki/дизайн-система-ui.md`, `docs/wiki/ввод-и-контроллеры.md`; тесты — `tests/check_team_catalog.gd`, `tests/check_controller_select_ui.gd`, `tests/check_team_select_view.gd`, `tests/check_team_select_ui.gd`.
- **Выбор форм:** `scenes/ui/kit_select_view.tscn` (`KitSelectView`) — общий экран из прототипа: две панели HOME/AWAY с каруселью 4 комплектов (`main/white/black/reserve` → HOME/AWAY/THIRD/FOURTH), лого/имя команды, счётчик, `READY!`, и средняя колонка с 3D-превью матча (статичный `SubViewport`, поле + 22 тела `gpf_fullbody`, broadcast-камера); home по умолчанию main, away — самый контрастный к форме хозяев. Локальный адаптер — `scenes/kit_select.tscn` (`TeamCatalog`/`ShellState`), сетевой — `scenes/network_kit_select.tscn` (локальный шаг между Teams и Match Hub; киты не синхронизируются, поэтому каждый пир правит обе формы как в 1P: home → away). Управление: `Left/Right` — комплект, `A/Enter` — ready, `B/Esc` — снять/назад; в 1P человек по очереди выбирает home и away, в 2P — каждое устройство свою, в сети — обе формы локально. Мышь инертна, шапки нет. Выбор идёт в `ShellState.kit_home/away` и в `MatchPackage.team.kit`; `GpfActors` применяет текстуру. Вики — `docs/wiki/дизайн-система-ui.md`, `docs/wiki/пакет-матча.md`; тесты — `tests/check_kit_select_view.gd`, `tests/check_kit_select_ui.gd`, `tests/check_kit_texture.gd`; скриншоты — `tests/capture_kit_select_shots.gd`.
- **Пакет матча:** `MatchPackage` (`scripts/data/match_package.gd`) собирает пакет из выбранных команд (весь состав, 22 итоговых стата, 11 нормализованных позиций/ролей, тактики, цвета, `logo_url`/`kit_url`, `kit`, опции длины/сложности). `scenes/prematch.tscn` (`scripts/ui/prematch.gd`) — тонкий локальный адаптер общего `MatchHubView` (`scenes/ui/match_hub_view.tscn`): две колонки 11 стартовых, scoreboard с рейтингом, command-ячейки **START MATCH / TEAM MANAGEMENT / SYSTEM SETTINGS / EXIT**; Game Plan — `GpfGamePlan` (порт `GamePlanPage`, тот же экран открывается из паузы матча), Options — difficulty + match length (5/7/10/12/15 минут реального матча, дефолт 10; в пакет уходит `duration = (minutes - 4.5) / 18`), Exit — выход в главное меню; локально 2P Start — голос каждого устройства. Сетевой адаптер — `scenes/network_match_hub.tscn` (`scripts/ui/network_match_hub.gd`): host-only options/Start, client ждёт MatchSetup, Game Plan локальный (составы не синхронизируются). `gpf_slice.gd` кладёт пакет в `GpfRuntimeNode.set_match_package()` до `restart_match()`. Встроенной пары команд нет: без пакета `gpf_slice` возвращает в выбор команд; пакет каждый раз собирается заново на `Start match`. C++ строит `MatchData` прямо из `TeamDataRaw` (без SQL) и больше не читает базу для матча; `start_match()` без пакета возвращает `false`. Пакет переживает F4/второй матч в узле runtime. ## Architecture
- **Autoload:** `FootballConstants` (see `project.godot` [autoload])
- **Entry point:** `main_menu.tscn` → `_on_start` loads `match.tscn` (NOT `match.tscn` directly)
- **All match logic** lives in `match_manager.gd` (controls input, AI, dribbling, goals, camera)
- **AI scripts** are child `Brain` components (`scripts/ai/brain.gd`, `class_name Brain extends Node`), not `set_script` on the body: `simple_ai.gd` (opponent), `teammate_ai.gd` (our team AI), `keeper_ai.gd` (goalkeeper in the Home goal) all `extends Brain` and read their body via `_body := get_parent()`. The body root itself is `scripts/player/player.gd` (`class_name Player extends CharacterBody3D`, has `brain()`). `MatchManager._ai_of(body)` is the bridge every manager call-site uses to reach an AI's fields/methods.
- **Player visuals** are a separate presentation layer: each field player gets a `scenes/player_visual.tscn` (`PlayerVisual`) child holding a rigged Mixamo model + AnimationTree. Gameplay (physics/AI) and presentation (model/anim) are kept decoupled.
- **Player locomotion** is a third component: every field player also gets a `PlayerMotor` child (`player_motor.gd`), spawned AFTER `PlayerVisual` (order matters — it finds its sibling visual once in `_ready()`). It owns all movement physics; callers only call `motor.set_move_intent(dir, speed_scale)`, never write `global_position`/`rotation` directly.
- **Ball physics** in `ball_controller.gd` — `BallState` machine (OPEN/TRAPPED/FLIGHT/CAUGHT), continuous **lead-follow** dribbling (NOT velocity-matching/spring), FLIGHT-gated ball↔player collision (source of truth).

## Key quirks
- **InputMap** is set up **programmatically** in `match_manager.gd:_setup_inputs()` — `project.godot` input bindings are unused/overridden. Always edit there, not in `project.godot`.
- **Player movement** is **velocity + `move_and_slide()`** via `PlayerMotor` (accel/decel, smoothed turn, smoothed lean/banking, sprint) — NOT direct position manipulation anymore. The slide tackle is still the one exception, moving the tackler via manual `move_and_collide()` while that player's motor is control-locked.
- **Slide tackle & fall system** (`match_manager.gd`, `TackleState`/`FallState` enums): `_tackle_state`/`_tackle_player` are single match-wide fields, not per-player — always check `controlled_player == _tackle_player` before treating "a tackle is happening" as "my tackle." Two separate hit radii: wide `TackleArea` (1.5m) only reaches the ball now; a much tighter `SLIDE_TACKLE_HIT_RADIUS` (0.9m, polled in `_tackle_slide()`) is what actually knocks a player down, so falls track real capsule contact instead of triggering from a visible gap. Tackle aim uses simple lead-prediction off the target's velocity (target usually = ball, which lead-follows its dribbler). Knockdowns are not yet fouls (no free kick). Victim fall is **animation-driven** (`fallen_idle` → roll → `standing_up`), not physics ragdoll — `ragdoll_skeleton.gd` and its tests still exist but are dead code, nothing live calls them.
- **Sprint:** hold Shift (or right trigger, analog) — human only, no stamina, just a higher `speed_scale` into `set_move_intent`.
- **Passing** (`match_manager.gd` + `scripts/match/pass_system.gd`, `scripts/match/pass_params.gd`): five pass types share the shot's charge bar via `enum ChargeAction { NONE, SHOT, PASS_SHORT, PASS_THROUGH, PASS_LOB, PASS_WALL, PASS_THROUGH_AIR }`. Controls: `D`/gamepad-X = shot, `X`/`A` = short pass, `W`/`Y` = through ball, `A`/`B` = lob, `Q`/`LB` held with short/through = wall-pass/through-air. `Q`/`LB` alone swaps player, but **only when our team doesn't have the ball** — with the ball it's the pass-combo modifier instead. `PassSystem` (`class_name PassSystem extends Object`) is 8 pure `static` functions (`select_target`, `lead_point`, `launch_ground`, `ground_pass_speed`, `launch_lob`, `interception_time`, `scatter_degrees`, `apply_scatter`) — never reads `FootballConstants`, tested by `tests/check_pass_system_math.gd`. **Ground-pass speed is distance-derived, not fixed:** `ground_pass_speed(distance, charge_ratio, ...)` computes `speed = distance / travel_time` where charge lerps `travel_time` (full charge = short time = fast; empty charge = long time = slow) — a pass always reaches its target regardless of charge, charge only changes how fast it arrives (added after playtest found fixed-power passes too weak over distance). `ball.launch(velocity)` is the impulse path for passes (vs. `ball.kick(dir, power)` for shots); `_action_power < 0.0` is the sentinel distinguishing them in `_on_action_contact`. `ball.peek_dribble_direction()` is a read-only twin of `get_dribble_direction()` — use it for anything called every frame (e.g. the telegraph marker); the mutating original races `_integrate_forces()`'s dribble-velocity tracking if called per-frame instead of once at a discrete moment. Receive-assist **unconditionally** steers a human receiver's stick toward an inbound ball (no "steer away = dummy run" carve-out anymore — that made receivers run the wrong way right after a handoff, since the stick was still aimed for the previous player). Give-and-go (`PASS_WALL`) is the one place AI sprints (`giving_run` group, `teammate_ai.gd`'s `begin_give_and_go`); honest interception (`simple_ai.gd`'s `begin_intercept`) uses real corridor geometry + a reaction delay + a miss chance, not omniscience. `teammate_ai.gd`'s `Role.RECEIVING` is live but currently unreachable (only 2 outfield players on our side; control always hands off to the receiver).
- **Collision layers are split three ways** — default layer (pitch + ball, for goal/tackle-`Area3D` detection), player layer (`PLAYER_COLLISION_MASK`, bit 2), boundary-wall layer (`BOUNDARY_COLLISION_LAYER`, bit 3). Players mask in player+boundary but NOT the ball's layer. **Ball↔player collision is FLIGHT-gated**: the ball adds the player bit to its mask only while in `FLIGHT` (shot/pass → block/intercept) and drops it on trap/loose (so a dribbled/picked-up ball is transparent to capsules — avoids the juddery-dribbling/spawn-shove bug). `continuous_cd` on the ball.
- **Dribbling** = continuous **lead-follow** in `_integrate_forces` (ball held ahead of the dribbler, distance grows with speed; breaks away past `DRIBBLE_CHASE_DIST` → player auto-chases; stick sets lead direction; sprint adds distance + random imprecision). Old velocity-matching/spring is gone.
- **Ground bounce is MANUAL** in `_integrate_forces` (material `bounce = 0.0`): the solver's restitution vs. the materialless `WorldBoundary` floor was killing the vertical → lobs "landed in a puddle." Now the ball tracks `_prev_vy` and, on ground contact after a descent, reflects vy by `BALL_BOUNCE` (0.6) + bleeds horizontal by `BALL_BOUNCE_FRICTION` (0.9) so it bounces and rolls on with decay; settles below `BALL_BOUNCE_MIN_SPEED`. Skipped for `_flat_flight`.
- **Kick/pass** = commit-action deferred impulse system. Resolution lives in `scripts/match/action_executor.gd` (`class_name ActionExecutor extends Node`, extracted from `match_manager` in Фаза 3a — a companion node like `PenaltyController`/`FreeKickController`): `_fire_charge()` (still in the manager, charge-as-timer) dispatches into `_action_executor.fire_shot()` (shot) / `.fire_pass()` (all 5 pass types), which set `_action_player`/`_action_power`/`_kick_action_active=true` (now executor fields), trigger animation (currently `pass` clip for both), defer `ball.kick()`/`ball.launch()` to `PlayerVisual.action_contact` — the factory still wires that signal to `match_manager._on_action_contact`, now a one-line forwarder into `_action_executor.on_action_contact()`. No `set_control_locked()` during kick/pass — the manager reads `_action_executor.is_kick_action_active()` (a getter, not a bare field) to skip the motor-lock early-return in `_handle_player_input`. See *Passing* bullet above for the pass-specific details.
- **Shot charge:** hold `D`/gamepad-X (max 0.5s), release fires. PowerBar (green→red gradient) visible during charge. Auto-fire at max charge. The aimed shot is in: `ActionExecutor.fire_shot` + `ShotSystem` do **continuous horizontal aim** (centre when facing straight, near-corner as the stick tilts), a **short-tap ground shot** (`< SHOT_GROUND_CHARGE_MAX` → flat/low, no landing bounce), a **chip** (`SHOT_CHIP`, ballistic lob), and a **curl** (`SHOT_CURL`, Magnus via `ball.launch_curl`). `SHOT_*` constants in `football_constants.gd`. Passes reuse the bar with a shorter 0.6s max.
- **Animation timing:** `PlayerVisual.ACTION_TIMING` dict — `kick`: `{contact: 0.35, lock: 0.5, speed: 1.0}`, `pass`: `{contact: 0.2, lock: 0.4, speed: 1.5}`. `action_contact` emitted at contact time, `action_finished` at lock time.
- **Camera:** sideline broadcast style — `camera_pivot` at X=-40, Y=20, follows ball Z, `look_at(Vector3(0,0,ballZ), UP)`. **DEBUG toggle:** `1` = broadcast (default), `3` = third-person follow behind `controlled_player` (`CAMERA_TP_*` constants) — for easier manual playtesting; doesn't affect penalty/free-kick's own fixed cameras.
- **Movement** is arrows/left-stick, camera-relative (uses `camera_pivot.global_transform.basis`) — WASD letters are freed for pass/shot actions, see *Passing* bullet above.
- **Controlled player** switch: **Q**/`LB` (manual, only when our team doesn't have the ball — otherwise it's the pass-combo modifier), auto-switch to whoever on our team has the ball
- **Players are spawned via one seam:** `PlayerFactory.spawn(config: PlayerConfig, team: Team)` (`scripts/match/player_factory.gd`) — instantiates `scenes/player.tscn`, tints kit, sets layers/groups/role, registers into a `Team` roster node, attaches the AI as a **child `Brain` component** (`add_child`, not `set_script`). No more `player_home`/`player_teammate`/`player_away` singletons — query `_team_home`/`_team_away` (`Team.players()`/`by_role()`/`keeper()`/`outfield()`) or groups instead. `_human_player` is the one remaining singleton (the human's default controlled body); it gets a `teammate_ai.gd` brain too, and self-gates on `controlled_player == _body`. The keeper is special-cased: `match_manager._keeper`/`penalty_controller._keeper`/`free_kick_controller._keeper` stay the body (freeze/exclusion checks), a parallel `_keeper_brain` field carries its API (`goal_line_z`, `on_ball_contact()`, `set_penalty_mode()`, etc.).

## Goalkeeper
- AI keeper in the Home goal (`match_manager._setup_keeper`): line-tracking, shot save (central catch / dive to a corner / tip over the bar), ball-in-hands, distribution. `keeper_ai.gd` drives it; **all geometry/timing math is pure functions in `keeper_logic.gd`** (`class_name KeeperLogic`, never reads `FootballConstants`, headless-tested).
- **Shot prediction is drag-aware** — `KeeperLogic.shot_intercept` integrates the ball's real drag + gravity (a naive `t=dz/vz` line over-estimated height and conceded on-target shots as "over the bar"). Central catches fire a `KEEPER_CATCH_LEAD`/`KEEPER_MISS_LEAD` before arrival (arms up in time) but only stick at the tight `KEEPER_REACH`. High central band (2.0–2.5m) is a per-shot coin-flip (`KEEPER_HIGH_CATCH_CHANCE`) catch-vs-tip.
- Ball in hands = `BallState.CAUGHT`, pinned to the **right-hand bone** (`PlayerVisual.get_hold_attachment`); a caught ball can't score.
- **Distribution: three variants, one wired at a time** (`_hold()`'s exit call selects) — overhand throw (**active**, arced, drag-compensated via `KeeperLogic.drag_horizontal_speed`), placing-ball→field-dribble→field-pass, and low roll / drop-kick. The rest are kept as building blocks for the real game AI.
- Two `ball_controller.gd` fixes it needed: **pending impulse applied at the start of `_integrate_forces`** (so a standstill shot isn't declared flight-ended on frame 1) and a **`KICK_GRACE_MSEC` self-collision exception** (so a point-blank strike isn't blocked by its own striker).
- Constants: `FootballConstants` `KEEPER_*`. Tests: `check_keeper_logic.gd`, `check_keeper_clear.gd`, `check_kick_grace.gd`. `DEBUG_DISABLE_OPPONENT`/`DEBUG_DISABLE_TEAMMATE` reverted to normal play; still on debug scaffolding: file logging, `[KEEPER]` prints — strip before merge.

## Penalty (Phase A)
- Press **P** to stage a penalty into the attacked goal. `scripts/match/penalty_controller.gd` (base, one penalty, `SETUP→AIM→STRIKE`, emits `struck`, `release_after_strike`) + pure math in `scripts/match/penalty_logic.gd` (`class_name PenaltyLogic`, never reads `FootballConstants`, headless-tested). Yellow world-space reticle on the goal plane (eases back to center when the stick is released); hold **kick** to charge power (ring = spread grows with power); `combo_modifier`+kick = chip. Direct = `aim + uniform sample in spread disc` (can miss). **Chip: power = landing distance** (short/in/over), horizontal drag-compensated via `KeeperLogic.drag_horizontal_speed`. Direct+chip only.
- Keeper dives **blind** into a 1-of-5 zone at contact (`keeper_ai.set_penalty_mode`/`begin_penalty_dive(zone, launch_vel)`). **Catchier (as-built):** on a **correct-side** guess the dive targets the ball's real drag-aware crossing point (`KeeperLogic.shot_intercept` on `launch_vel`, since `ball.linear_velocity` is still 0 that frame) with no `KEEPER_SAVE_ERROR` (`_begin_save(..., no_error=true)`), so a right guess reliably reaches even a corner; a **wrong side still misses**. CENTER stays central and resolves by the existing height reflex.
- **K (`keeper_dive_debug`) — penalty into OUR goal, human plays our keeper:** team_2 outfielder (`AIKickerIntent`) shoots at the team_1 goal (`+field_length`); human controls our keeper (`Role.KEEPER`, `HumanKeeperIntent` with `lat_sign = _into` so stick-right = screen-right for the `+Z` goal). `controlled_player` untouched; `_clear_box()` keeps **both** keepers (`role_gk`) in their goals.
- After the strike the ball goes **straight back to normal play**; a goal is a **Law-10 whole-ball check** (`GoalArea` `body_entered` only *arms* it → `match_manager._confirm_goal` polls until the ball's centre is `BALL_RADIUS` past the line, cancelling if the keeper catches/parries it back out) → confirmed goal ends in a kickoff. No RESOLVE/timeout in the base controller (that's the future shootout layer). `match_manager` gates input/AI/camera via `_penalty_active`.
- **Root-motion run-up (A1):** `penalty_kick_l/_r` kept out of `IN_PLACE_CLIPS`; `PlayerVisual.consume_root_motion()` returns per-frame advance in game meters (raw Hips track × `PEN_ROOT_SCALE`, calibrated by `tools/measure_penalty_runup.gd`); controller pre-places the kicker `PEN_RUNUP_DIST` back and drives the body along `_forward`. Re-run the measure tool if the clips are re-exported. **Gotcha:** `root_motion_track` is set only for the penalty clip inside `trigger()` (global broke `keeper_idle`/dive vertical) and cleared only **after** the return crossfade (`get_fading_from_node()` empty; clearing at `lock` slid the body backward into idle). Kick→idle return is a real crossfade, not `AT_END`.
- Constants: `FootballConstants` `PEN_*`. Tests: `check_penalty_logic.gd`, `check_penalty_flow.gd` (smoke). Phases B (save as keeper) / C (shootout) are spec-only. Interactive feel needs a human run. Spec/plan under `docs/superpowers/`.
