## Format

- Premier League: 12 clubs, only the last is relegated (`result12=REL`). No split, no play-off for the
  title; the champion is the table winner.
- First League: second level, dominated by reserve sides of Premier League clubs. Reserve teams cannot
  be promoted, so the highest-placed eligible club takes the promotion place and clubs sometimes go up
  from mid-table. Promotion also needs a licence.
- First League relegation is to the Armenian Amateur A-League, outside our data.

## Conflicts

Our ARM2 row says 15 clubs; the league's own article says 16 (`teams = 16`) but is dated to the
2025-26 season and its `current` link points at 2025-26. No 2026-27 source was found, so 15 is kept
(our row) and 16 is recorded as the last confirmed figure. It is plausible that one First League club
moved up with the Premier League expansion, but that is not confirmed.

## Oddities

- The Premier League grew from 10 to 12 one season after it had already absorbed a disqualification
  (West Armenia in 2025-26). Sizes in this pyramid move for administrative reasons more often than for
  sporting ones.
- A 12-club league with a single relegation place and a reserve-team-heavy second level makes the
  Armenian pyramid the most "closed" of the countries in this batch in practice: the bottom club of
  level 1 usually has no eligible replacement pressing from below.
