# San Marino — football pyramid, 2026-27

San Marino has **one national league and nothing below it**. There is no second division, no
promotion and no relegation: a club cannot leave the Campionato Sammarinese except by folding, and
a new club can only enter by being admitted. That makes the pyramid a single closed box, not a
ladder.

The 2026-27 season is the 42nd edition, started **28 August 2026**, and is played by **16 clubs**:
the 15 ordinary Sammarinese clubs plus **San Marino Academy U22**, the federation's under-22 side.
All 16 play in one group, home and away, 30 rounds. Clubs have **no home grounds** — the venue for
each match is drawn from a short list of pitches (Serravalle, Dogana, Fiorentino, Domagnano,
Montegiardino, Acquaviva, Serravalle B).

## Level 1 — Campionato Sammarinese

- **Title is decided by the table.** Since 2023-24 the old double-elimination championship
  play-off is gone: whoever finishes 1st after 30 rounds is champion (2025-26: Tre Fiori).
- **European play-off, not a title play-off.** Places 2-11 enter a separate knock-out that decides
  the second UEFA Conference League place: 8th-11th play a single-match preliminary round, 2nd-7th
  go straight to the quarter-finals, quarter-finals and semi-finals are two-legged, and the 2nd-3rd
  and 4th-5th placing finals are single matches. The champions take the Champions League place.
- **No relegation.** Bottom of the table simply finishes last. The same 16 clubs are expected back
  the following season.
- **San Marino Academy U22** is a development side, not a normal club, and has finished 14th twice
  (2023-24, 2025-26). If the simulation treats every row as an equal club, it can win the league.

## Caveats

- There is no English Wikipedia page for 2026-27; the Italian page is the primary source and the
  English league article is one season behind (it still shows 2025-26 as current).
- The Italian text says the **2nd-placed club** takes the Conference League place, while the
  English season article says the **play-off winner** does. Recorded in `conflicts`; for a
  simulation the practical rule is "one extra European place decided by the 2-11 play-off".
- With no relegation, any "bottom club goes down" logic must be switched off for this country.
