# Thailand — football pyramid, 2026-27

Season runs August 2026 → May 2027. **Not a calendar-year season** — the Thai League moved to
autumn/spring in 2020-21 and the 2026-27 season is the current one.

| Level | Competition | Clubs | Shape | Rounds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Thai League 1 (T1) | 16 | double round-robin | 30 |
| 2 | Thai League 2 (T2) | 18 | double round-robin | 34 |

Both sizes match our rows (16 / 18) and both levels are right.

## Level 1 — Thai League 1

16 clubs, 30 rounds, no split. The bottom three are relegated **directly** — there is no
relegation play-off. 2024-25 ended with Khon Kaen United, Nakhon Pathom United and Nongbua
Pitchaya going down as 14th, 15th and 16th; 2025-26 sent down the same shape of three.

## Level 2 — Thai League 2

18 clubs, 34 rounds. Promotion is **2 direct + 1 play-off place**, not three direct:

- champion and runner-up go up;
- 3rd to 6th enter a promotion play-off for the third place (2025-26: Pattani won it after
  finishing outside the top two; 2024-25: Kanchanaburi Power).

The bottom three are relegated to Thai League 3 (level 3, not in our rows), which is itself
regional (three regions).

## Boundary as numbers

| Boundary | Up | of which play-off | Down | of which play-off | Allocation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 ↔ 2 | 3 | 1 | 3 | 0 | free |

Both directions settle on the table except the single T2 play-off place, so the boundary is
"3 up (1 via play-off) / 3 down (all direct)".

## Data problems in our rows

Nothing wrong in our two rows. The only correction is contextual: the task note calls Thailand a
calendar-year league, which was true up to 2019 but has not been since 2020-21.

## Confidence

High. Sizes, rounds and the 2+1 promotion shape are stated in the current league articles and
confirmed by the 2025-26 and 2026-27 transfer lists.
