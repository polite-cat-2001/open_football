# Moldova — football pyramid, 2026-27

Moldova runs an August–May season. Cups and UEFA competitions are out of scope. Both of our levels
have a **two-phase shape**, and both are open at the boundary between them.

## Level 1 — Super Liga

- 8 clubs. **Phase I is 21 rounds**: each pair meets twice, then a third time, so every club plays 21.
- The **bottom two are relegated immediately at the end of Phase I** and do not play on — in 2026-27
  Bălți were already down by 8 November.
- The top six play **Phase II** (10 more matches), carrying **half their Phase I points, rounded down**.
  Champion and UEFA places come from Phase II.
- So a "league table" for this division only makes sense once you know which phase you are in.

## Level 2 — Liga 1

- **11 clubs in two geographic groups** for 2026-27: Group A 6 clubs, Group B 5.
- After Phase I the top two of each group go into **Phase II Group 1**, everyone else into Group 2.
- Promotion: the official Liga 1 summary says **the top two are promoted**. In 2026-27 the two Super
  Liga relegations land directly in "Liga 1 Phase II", which is consistent with two going up.
- Bottom two go down to Liga 2.
- Zimbru-2 are in Group A but are marked ineligible for Phase II Group 1 — a reserve team again.

## Caveats

- The Super Liga's Phase I / Phase II point halving is unusual; if the sim keeps a single table it will
  disagree with the real final standings.
- The 11-club Liga 1 means one group of five, so clubs in Group B play two fewer Phase I matches.
