# Ghana — football pyramid, 2026-27

One level only in our database: the **Ghana Premier League**. Below it sits the Division One League
with three regional zones, which is a real second tier but is not in our rows, so nothing can be
pitched against it.

## Level 1 — Ghana Premier League

- 18 clubs, 34 rounds. Season runs September to May; the 2026-27 edition started on 5 September 2026.
- **Relegation:** the bottom three go down, and each is placed into **its own regional zone** of the
  Division One League (Northern, Middle, Southern) — the drop is geographical, not a free choice.
- **Promotion into the league:** three clubs come up, one per zone (2026-27 intake was three).
- No title play-off: the champion is whoever tops the table. No relegation play-out.
- Continental places (CAF Champions League / Confederation Cup) are out of scope here.

## Caveats

- The league is the only national division; everything under it is zonal and its shape changes with
  entries, so a "pyramid" model should stop at level 1 rather than invent a second tier.
- Licensing and club withdrawals have repeatedly changed the size mid-season in past years (the
  league has run with 16, 18 and 20 clubs within the last decade); 18 is the 2026-27 figure.
- 2026-27 was still in its first months on 20 November 2026, so the relegation places are the rule,
  not an outcome.
