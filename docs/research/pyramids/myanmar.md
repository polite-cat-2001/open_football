# Myanmar — football pyramid, 2026-27

**Myanmar is not calendar-year any more.** The league article states seasons "typically run from August
to May", 22 matches per club, and the current competition is the 2026-27 season. The ticket said
"calendar year 2026" — that was true of the old Myanmar National League; flagged as a conflict.

The league was also **rebranded in August 2026**: Myanmar National League → **M League** (Grand Royal
M League for sponsorship). Our name is already correct.

## Level 1 — M League

- 12 clubs, 22 rounds, three points for a win, head-to-head before goal difference if needed.
- **Bottom two relegated to the M2 League**, top two promoted from it. No play-off.
- 2026-27 field: ten survivors plus **Chinland** and **Myawady** up from the M2.
- Shan United have won seven titles (2017, 2019, 2020, 2022, 2023, 2024-25, 2025-26) and went unbeaten
  in five of them. That is a strength problem for the sim, not a format problem.

## Caveats

- The 2026-27 season page has no independent league table yet (the article carries 2026-27 fixtures but
  not results), so the size/format come from the league article: medium confidence.
- Club licensing is weak: the 2019-2026 period saw clubs fold, withdraw and be replaced mid-pyramid.
  A promotion/relegation model should tolerate a 12-club league with a variable bottom.
