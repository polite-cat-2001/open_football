# Malaysia — football pyramid, 2026-27

**Malaysia is not a calendar-year league any more.** The 2025-26 season ran 8 August 2025 to 17 May
2026 and 2026-27 runs 28 August 2026 to 23 May 2027, so this report uses the 2026-27 season like any
European league. That contradicts the note in the ticket — flagged.

## Level 1 — Super League

- 12 clubs in 2026-27: eleven from 2025-26 plus Kelantan Red Warrior, promoted from the A1.
- Kelantan The Real Warriors and PDRM were **ejected** for failing the national licence, and
  Immigration rebranded as Star City. That is how the league went 13 → 12.
- **Relegation:** the regulation number is two, but Malaysia has not actually relegated a club on
  form for years — places are lost through licensing. Treat the two downward places as contingent.

## Level 2 — A1 Semi-Pro League

- 14 clubs, 26 rounds. The 2026-27 competition format says plainly: **top two promoted**, bottom two
  relegated to the A2 Amateur League, top three at mid-season qualify for the Malaysia Cup.
- **Reserve/feeder sides cannot go up** — JDT II, Selangor II, Immigration II and Negeri Sembilan II
  are marked "ineligible for promotion: parent team in Super League / developmental team". So if one
  of them finishes top two, the promotion place slides to the next eligible club.
- 2025-26 actually promoted three clubs (Melaka and Immigration to the Super League as debutants, and
  the league also sent Kelantan Red Warrior up a year later) — promotion is by application and
  licence as much as by table.

## Caveats

- The Super League's "2 down" is a regulation, not an observed outcome; licensing ejections are more
  common than sporting relegation.
- DPMM (Brunei) play in the league as an invited club and are ineligible for AFC competitions; that
  does not affect promotion/relegation.
